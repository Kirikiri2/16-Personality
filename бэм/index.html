What it is?
The Myers–Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI) is a personality assessment created by Katharine Cook Briggs and her daughter Isabel Briggs Myers in the mid-20th century. It was inspired by Carl Jung’s theory of psychological types. The purpose of MBTI is not to diagnose people but to help them understand their natural preferences in thinking, decision-making, and interacting with others.
